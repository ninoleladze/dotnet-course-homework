Summary of the exercise (correlated subqueries against the supermarket
domain in marketplace.db):

- task1.sql: cities with no streets — NOT EXISTS correlated on
  street.city_id = city.id.
- task2.sql: product categories where the category has products and every
  one of them has at least one purchase — EXISTS + NOT EXISTS/NOT EXISTS
  correlated on product_category.id.
- task3.sql: products priced above the average price of their own category —
  scalar correlated subquery on product_title.product_category_id.

All three queries were validated against CorrelatedSubqueries.Tests\
SqlTaskTests.cs (structural checks for SELECT/FROM/WHERE/ORDER BY/subquery
presence, plus row count, schema, column types, and exact data comparison
against the fixtures in CorrelatedSubqueries.Tests\Data\task1.csv,
task2.csv, task3.csv). Final test run: 33/33 SqlTaskTests passed.
